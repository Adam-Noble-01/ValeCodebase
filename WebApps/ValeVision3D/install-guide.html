Install the ValeVision 3D App

A step-by-step guide for Vale Garden Houses colleagues

Which device do you need help with?

Choose your device below and we will show you the exact steps — it only takes a minute.

Installing on Windows 11

Works in Microsoft Edge and Google Chrome. Takes about 30 seconds.

Your browser supports one-click install — press the button above and choose Install. Done!

1

Open ValeVision 3D in Edge or Chrome

You are probably already there if you are reading this page. If not, press the button below to open the app gallery.

Open ValeVision 3D Gallery

2

Click the install icon in the address bar

Look at the right-hand end of the address bar at the top of your browser. You will see a small icon of a screen with a down-arrow. Click it and choose Install.

3

Can't see the icon? Use the browser menu instead

Click the three dots in the top-right corner of the browser, then choose Apps → Install ValeVision 3D (Edge) or Cast, save and share → Install page as app (Chrome).

4

Pin it to your taskbar

The app now opens in its own window and appears in your Start menu. Right-click the ValeVision 3D icon on the taskbar while it is open and choose Pin to taskbar so it is always one click away.

Installing on iPhone & iPad

Apple devices install web apps through Safari's "Add to Home Screen" feature.

Important — you must use Safari Other browsers on iPhone and iPad cannot install the app. If this page opened in a different app, copy the link into Safari first.
1

Open ValeVision 3D in Safari

Press the button below to open the app gallery in this browser tab.

Open ValeVision 3D Gallery

2

Tap the Share button

It is the square with an arrow pointing up. On iPad it sits at the top-right next to the address bar; on iPhone it is at the bottom-centre of the screen.

3

Tap "Add to Home Screen"

Scroll down the list that appears until you see Add to Home Screen (a square with a plus sign) and tap it.

4

Tap "Add" — you're done

Tap Add in the top-right corner. The ValeVision 3D icon now appears on your home screen like a normal app.

5

Always open the app from the home screen icon

Apple does not allow links in emails to open the app automatically — they will always open a web browser instead. For the full-screen app experience, start from the ValeVision 3D icon on your home screen every time.

Installing on Android

Works in Google Chrome and Samsung Internet. Takes about 30 seconds.

Your browser supports one-tap install — press the button above and choose Install. Done!

1

Open ValeVision 3D in Chrome

Press the button below to open the app gallery.

Open ValeVision 3D Gallery

2

Tap the three-dot menu

Tap the three dots in the top-right corner of Chrome to open the browser menu.

3

Tap "Add to Home screen" then "Install"

Choose Add to Home screen (on some phones this says Install app), then confirm with Install. The ValeVision 3D icon appears on your home screen.

After installing — finding your project

Your notification email tells you what to look for Open the ValeVision 3D app from your home screen, Start menu or taskbar, then find your project in the gallery using the project code (e.g. 63569) or the project name from the email. New projects appear automatically — no links needed.

Any problems? Contact the Visualisation Department and we will get you up and running.
