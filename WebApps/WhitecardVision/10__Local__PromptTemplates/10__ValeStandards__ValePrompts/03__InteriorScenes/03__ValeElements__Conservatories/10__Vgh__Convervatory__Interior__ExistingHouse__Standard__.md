**FINISHES WITHIN THE SPACE**

**Tiled Floor**
- The floor is tiled, the floor tiles are large format natural stone pavers characterised by a honed finish in a neutral limestone palette. The layout follows a traditional flagstone pattern with staggered grout lines typical of period properties. Minor surface variations should exist for realism.

**Interior Walls Left & Low Walls**

- The Low walls beneath the conservatory window cills are plastered and painted Farrow & Ball Colour "Ammonite".

**Interior End Stone Wall**
- The Wall on the far end is a Feature Natural Stonework Interior Wall.

**Classic Joinery**
- Tall 175mm skirting boards with a moulded top edge are painted to match the white paint of the orangery.
- Window boards with a moulded top edge are painted to match the white paint of the orangery.
- The opening through to the next room on the right side wall is lined with panelling withing the opening reveal and has a classic architrave around the sides and top of the opening.
