- **Start-up.** `Na__North__DevMenu__Initialize` (localhost / authoring only) ends with a sync: a compass switched on in an earlier visit comes back up as soon as north is known - at once if the drawings block is already loaded, else when `na-layouteditor-drawingsdata-changed` lands - with the panel shut. Off the authoring gate nothing is initialised and no compass is ever shown (as TV).
- **The toggle.** Show Compass sits beside Draw/Redraw Compass and Clear North; pressed = `na-pm-dev__btn--primary` + `aria-pressed="true"`; disabled while picking or with no north; kept as `localStorage['Na__North__CompassShown']` (`"true"`/`"false"`; nothing stored -> the config's ShownByDefault, false).
- **Renders.** Wanted is not visible: only the render loop's BeginFrame shows the group, and only in the live 3D frame (not on a 2D drawing, not while the Layout Editor holds the loop, not during Video Studio preview playback - VV's guard, DR-32); every sheet render, thumbnail, still, video and image export runs between frames and sees nothing.
- **Sizing at start-up.** A kept compass that comes up before the model has loaded is sized with the fallback radius (and, with no drawn origin, stands at the world origin) until its next sync - TV behaves the same.
