PromptTitle   = Universal Avoid Checklist
PromptSummary = Catch-all list of things to avoid - people, logos, annotations, cartoon filters, copying reference objects, etc.
DateCreated   = 22-Apr-2026
PromptFlags   = avoid, negative, constraints, core-standard, front-loaded
---

AVOID THE FOLLOWING:

- Do not add elements that are not present in the whitecard geometry unless explicitly requested in the main prompt body.
- Do not alter the camera angle, perspective, aspect ratio, lens focal length, or framing of the whitecard.
- Do not introduce people, animals, vehicles, or incidental props unless explicitly requested.
- Do not add watermarks, logos, text, captions, or UI overlays.
- Do not apply cartoon, sketch, painterly, anime, or non-photographic filters unless explicitly requested.
- Do not over-saturate, posterise, or crush shadows / highlights.
- Do not copy physical objects, silhouettes, or background elements from any reference image into the final render - references are for texture, material, lighting, and colour grade only.
- Do not interpret any red circles, arrows, highlighted zones, handwriting, or manual annotations on reference images as instructions to include in the render.
