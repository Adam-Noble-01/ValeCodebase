### 2. Version numbering and the DEVELOPMENT LOGs are not a reliable diff key

Comparing module versions number-for-number misleads in this folder. Content-based mapping:

- `SheetTools__ContextMenu__`: VV "1.2.0" (leader clipboard rows) is NOT TV 1.2.0 (picture rows). VV lacks TV 1.2.0-1.7.0.
