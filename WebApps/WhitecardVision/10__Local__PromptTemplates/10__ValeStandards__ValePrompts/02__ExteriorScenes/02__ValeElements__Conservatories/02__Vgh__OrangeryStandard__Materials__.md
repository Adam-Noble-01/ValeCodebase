    CONSERVATORY ROOF

    - The Roof is a Monopitch Roof & Spans from the left to the right, the conservatory roof is doubled glazed panels set between the structural roof timbers.
    - The Roof timbers have a classic moulding to their underside edges.
    - On the right side of the roof at the apex where it meets the wall there are Roof Vents, The roof vents are opening based casements that open to ventilate the apex of the roof. These vents are in their closed positions.
    - Along the Apex there is a timber wall plate with a moulded bottom edge that the roof timbers are seated against.
    - Around the Eaves above the window and door panels there is a decorative traditional crown moulding profile featuring a sequence of stepped curves and square fillets and staff bead.
    - The conservatory Roof Features a lighting hook on a lighting block positioned roughly at the mid span of one of the structural roof timbers.
    - Note: there are two different kinds of roof timber, shallower in depth are the glaze bars and thicker in depth are the Structural Roof Supports such as the on the lighting block is affixed to.
