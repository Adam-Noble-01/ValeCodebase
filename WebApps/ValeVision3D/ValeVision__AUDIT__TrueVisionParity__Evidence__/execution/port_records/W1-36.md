- On the 3D tab, R, B, T, Y, V, 1-9, Page Up and Page Down do what they always did; on a drawing tab they do nothing to the hidden 3D view.
- Fly through the model and press Fly again to leave it: the 3D view stops redrawing when idle (it used to keep drawing every frame after one flight).
- Informational: on the 3D tab Page Up is the NEXT scene and Page Down the previous (both apps' key files say so), while on a drawing Page Down is the next drawing. That is TrueVision's existing binding; say if you want the 3D pair swapped (a TrueVision-lane question, DR-36).
