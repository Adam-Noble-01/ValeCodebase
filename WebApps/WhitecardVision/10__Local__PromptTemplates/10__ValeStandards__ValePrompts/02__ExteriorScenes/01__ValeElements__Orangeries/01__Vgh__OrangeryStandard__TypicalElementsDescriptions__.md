**ORANGERY ELEMENTS**

**Orangery Paint Colour**
- The Orangery is painted in a slightly off-white colour.
- The Orangery should not be painted in a car crash white colour, instead a soft white easy on the eyes conveying timeless white Orangery paint.

**Orangery Door Cills**
- The Orangery Door Cills are varnished timber.
- The Orangery Door Cills are Sepele Timber, Avoid applying any paint finishing to the timber door cills on the Orangery.

**Orangery Window Cills & Low Walls**
- The Orangery **Window Cills Are Painted** Avoid painting the Cills only where the doors are on the orangery.
- The Orangery Low Walls should match the host dwelling in appearance, all Orangery dwarf wall finishes, textures etc should be identical to those used on the existing house.

**Orangery Columns**
- The Orangery has square unadorned Tuscan style pilasters with simple structural capitals.
- The Orangery Columns are painted in a matching paint colour to the Orangery paint.

**Orangery Entablature**
- The Orangery features classical entablature features a flat frieze and a moulded projecting cornice/crown moulding with a lead capping to the top.

**Orangery Roof**
- The Orangery has a hipped glazed lantern roof inset from the entablature perimeter.

**Orangery Roof Finials**
- The Orangery has dark spherical ball style decorative roof finials positioned on the ridge ends

**Orangery Windows**
- The Orangery has tall multi pane timber casement windows featuring traditional slender graceful glazing bars.

**Orangery Doors - Right Hand Side**
- The Orangery has matching central multi pane French doors incorporating solid lower timber base panels.

**Orangery Doors - Left Hand Side**
- The Orangery has French doors incorporating solid lower timber base panels.
- The Orangery Doors have fielded panels with decorative a moulded beading detail around the perimeter of the door panels.

**Orangery Rainwater Downpipes**
- The Orangery has decorative rainwater hoppers painted to match the Orangery paint colour.
- The Orangery has external rainwater downpipes that are painted to match the Orangery paint colour.
