General Drawing Terms

The following notes, limitations and omissions apply to every drawing issued with this quotation. Where a drawing carries notes of its own, those notes are printed immediately after that drawing and are read in addition to these.

1. General Drawing Terms, Limitations and Omissions

  1. 1.01

    Status of the drawings at the stage they are issued, and confirmation of what has and has not been surveyed, checked or approved at that stage.

  2. 1.02

    Confirmation that figured dimensions govern and that no dimension is to be scaled from the drawing, whatever scale the sheet is issued at.

  3. 1.03

    The datum every level and setting out dimension on the sheet is measured from, and what is to be done where no datum is shown.

  4. 1.04

    Confirmation that the builders upstand, its dimensions and its tolerances are shown for information only and are the responsibility of others.

  5. 1.05

    Structural elements shown on the drawing which are indicative, and confirmation that no structural design of the supporting structure is implied by their appearance.

  6. 1.06

    Items shown on the drawing for context which do not form part of our supply, and how they are distinguished on the sheet.

  7. 1.07

    Services, drainage, ventilation ducting and electrical provision, and confirmation of what is excluded unless expressly stated in the quotation.

  8. 1.08

    Finishes, colours and glass appearance as drawn, and confirmation that the drawing is not a colour reference and that samples govern.

  9. 1.09

    The requirement to check every drawing against the site as built and against other trades' information before any work is put in hand.

  10. 1.10

    Revision control, superseded issues, and the obligation to work only from the latest revision of a sheet.

  11. 1.11

    Copyright and ownership of the drawings, and the purposes for which they may be reproduced or passed to others.

  12. 1.12

    The point at which a drawing becomes a manufacturing drawing, what changes that, and the point after which changes become chargeable.

Note on the status of these drawing terms

These drawing terms have not yet been reviewed or approved by Vale Garden Houses' legal advisers. They are issued as a working draft to describe the intended basis on which the drawings are read, and should not be relied upon as final wording.
