# General Drawing Terms

> EDITOR NOTE. Every clause below states its SUBJECT ONLY. None of it is legal
> wording and none of it has been drafted or reviewed by anyone qualified to draft
> it. Replace each subject line with Vale approved wording as it is settled.
>
> FORMAT. Every blank-line separated paragraph below is one clause and is numbered
> automatically. Lines starting with a chevron are editor notes and never print.
> Do not number clauses by hand; the section number comes from the config table.
>
> WHICH DOCUMENT THIS BELONGS TO. This file is part of the GENERAL DRAWING TERMS,
> which is its own document at the back of the issued pack and is numbered in its own
> series. It is not part of the business terms of engagement, and it is not one of the
> numbered sections of that document. The two are read by different people for
> different reasons: a contractor setting out on site reads this, a client signing an
> order reads the business terms.
>
> CONTEXT FOR WHOEVER DRAFTS THESE. These are the omissions and limitations that apply
> to every drawing in the pack, whichever lantern it shows. A lantern that also has
> notes of its own gets a Drawing Terms page of its own immediately after its sheet;
> these clauses still apply to it. This is where the titleblock QR code lands when a
> lantern has no notes of its own.

Status of the drawings at the stage they are issued, and confirmation of what has and
has not been surveyed, checked or approved at that stage.

Confirmation that figured dimensions govern and that no dimension is to be scaled from
the drawing, whatever scale the sheet is issued at.

The datum every level and setting out dimension on the sheet is measured from, and what
is to be done where no datum is shown.

Confirmation that the builders upstand, its dimensions and its tolerances are shown for
information only and are the responsibility of others.

Structural elements shown on the drawing which are indicative, and confirmation that no
structural design of the supporting structure is implied by their appearance.

Items shown on the drawing for context which do not form part of our supply, and how
they are distinguished on the sheet.

Services, drainage, ventilation ducting and electrical provision, and confirmation of
what is excluded unless expressly stated in the quotation.

Finishes, colours and glass appearance as drawn, and confirmation that the drawing is
not a colour reference and that samples govern.

The requirement to check every drawing against the site as built and against other
trades' information before any work is put in hand.

Revision control, superseded issues, and the obligation to work only from the latest
revision of a sheet.

Copyright and ownership of the drawings, and the purposes for which they may be
reproduced or passed to others.

The point at which a drawing becomes a manufacturing drawing, what changes that, and
the point after which changes become chargeable.
