# Builders Upstand Terms

>
> FORMAT. Every blank-line separated paragraph below is one clause and is numbered
> automatically. Lines starting with a chevron are editor notes and never print.
> Do not number clauses by hand; the section number comes from the config table.
>
> CONTEXT FOR WHOEVER DRAFTS THESE. The builders upstand is built by others, and the
> lantern is manufactured to the dimensions shown on the approved drawings. Almost
> every dispute on a lantern job starts here: the upstand as built is out of square,
> out of level, or a different size from the one the lantern was made to. These
> clauses are the ones that decide who carries that cost.

Confirmation that the builders upstand is constructed by others and does not form part
of our supply unless expressly stated in the quotation.

The dimension the stated lantern size is measured to, being the outer face of the
builders upstand, and the consequences of measuring to any other face.

Dimensional tolerance permitted on the completed upstand, expressed as a maximum
deviation on each stated dimension.

Squareness and diagonal tolerance permitted across the completed opening.

Level tolerance permitted across the top of the upstand, and the maximum acceptable
deviation over the full run of each side.

Structural adequacy of the upstand and the supporting structure, and the client's
responsibility for its design and for any structural engineer's involvement.

Requirement for the upstand to be complete, cured, dry and clear of obstruction before
the installation date is confirmed.

Weathering, flashing, damp proofing and insulation of the upstand, and confirmation of
where our responsibility begins and ends.

Consequences where the upstand as built falls outside the stated tolerances, including
remedial work, remanufacture, aborted visit costs and programme delay.

Site survey and check measure, when it is carried out, who attends, and the point after
which dimensional changes are chargeable.
