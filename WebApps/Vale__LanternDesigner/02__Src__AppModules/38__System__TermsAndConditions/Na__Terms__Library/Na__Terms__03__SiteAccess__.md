# Access to Site Terms

> EDITOR NOTE. Every clause below states its SUBJECT ONLY. None of it is legal
> wording and none of it has been drafted or reviewed by anyone qualified to draft
> it. Replace each subject line with Vale approved wording as it is settled.
>
> The terms document closes with a single notice saying the terms have not been
> reviewed. It is ReviewNoticeText in Na__Terms__Config.json, and switching
> ReviewNoticeEnabled to false is what removes it once the library is approved.
>
> FORMAT. Every blank-line separated paragraph below is one clause and is numbered
> automatically. Lines starting with a chevron are editor notes and never print.
> Do not number clauses by hand; the section number comes from the config table.

Vehicular access required to the property, including the largest vehicle that must
reach the unloading point and the headroom and width needed to do so.

Hard standing, ground conditions and the client's responsibility for a route capable
of carrying the delivery and any lifting plant.

Unloading arrangements, and who is responsible for providing labour or mechanical
handling at the point of delivery.

Craneage and mechanical lifting where required, who arranges it, who pays for it, and
the exclusion zone that must be available on the day.

Scaffolding, edge protection and safe working platforms, who provides them, and the
standard they must meet before our teams will work from them.

Site welfare, secure storage and the client's responsibility for materials once they
have been delivered.

Working hours, site rules and any restrictions arising from a party wall agreement,
conservation consent or a neighbour access agreement.

Aborted or delayed visit charges where access is not available as agreed on the
scheduled day.

Site readiness criteria that must be met before an installation date is confirmed,
and who confirms them.
