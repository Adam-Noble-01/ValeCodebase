# Payment Terms

> EDITOR NOTE. Every clause below states its SUBJECT ONLY. None of it is legal
> wording and none of it has been drafted or reviewed by anyone qualified to draft
> it. Replace each subject line with Vale approved wording as it is settled.
>
> The terms document closes with a single notice saying the terms have not been
> reviewed. It is ReviewNoticeText in Na__Terms__Config.json, and switching
> ReviewNoticeEnabled to false is what removes it once the library is approved.
>
> FORMAT. Every blank-line separated paragraph below is one clause and is numbered
> automatically. Lines starting with a chevron are editor notes and never print.
> Do not number clauses by hand; the section number comes from the config table.

Deposit percentage and the point at which it becomes payable on order confirmation.

Stage payment schedule, if any, and the milestones that trigger each stage.

Balance payment trigger, whether that is despatch from works, delivery to site or
completion of installation.

Payment period from invoice date, and the accepted payment methods.

Retention of title until payment has been received in full, including the position
where goods have been affixed to the property.

Interest, compensation and recovery costs applicable to late payment.

Our right to suspend manufacture, delivery or installation where a payment is overdue,
and the programme consequences of doing so.

Storage charges where a client is unable to accept delivery on the agreed date, and
the point from which they begin to accrue.

Value added tax treatment, including the evidence required for any reduced or zero
rating claimed by the client.

Cancellation charges and the recoverable costs at each stage of manufacture.
