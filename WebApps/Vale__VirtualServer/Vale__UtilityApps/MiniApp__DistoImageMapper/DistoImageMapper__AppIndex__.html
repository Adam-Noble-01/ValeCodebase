Disto Image Mapper

Open a DISTO Survey

Choose a survey folder and it becomes a 3D photo atlas: the points in their true positions, the plan and elevation on their planes, and every photo on its point.

Or drag the folder onto this window, or on a device without folders.

This folder holds several surveys

What a survey folder holds
3D_ .dxfThe 3D space: every point in true position
2DG_ .dxfThe plan, laid flat under the points
2DW_ .dxfThe elevation, stood up behind the points
.csvThe shot log: angles and distances, which locate the tripod
IMG_ .jpgThe photo taken at each point
Everything stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded.
