## 5. Behaviour as landed

- **PDF fonts.** The first PDF call of a session (`EnsureJsPdf`: the first drawing tab's PreloadMetrics, Download PDF,
  the specification download) fetches the three AD04 cuts once (about 390 KB); a host that is down makes it resolve false
  with one `[ValeVision3D LayoutEditor] PDF font unavailable:` warning per cut and Helvetica stays; the next call retries.
  The first-open veil already waits on that promise (TrueVision's design, W1-33) under its own cap.
- **Until W1-26** the files still print Helvetica (the chrome painter's choice) and are byte-for-byte the size they were;
  from W1-26 they carry Open Sans and grow by about 26-29 KB per weight used (preview: sheet 37 -> 54 KB, spec 2 -> 32 KB).
- **Screen.** Sheet chrome text now paints in Open Sans (Style FontFamily) while SheetChrome still measures in Helvetica
  until W1-26 - the state TrueVision's own screen is in before its fonts load. Measured with jsPDF's metrics
  (`logs/widths__helvetica_vs_opensans.txt`): Open Sans is NARROWER for the uppercase labels and captions (0.87-0.98) but
  WIDER for mixed-case title-block values (up to 1.062: "Mr & Mrs Featherstonehaugh" 30.19 mm vs 28.42 mm; the address
  1.031), so in that interim a value fitted tightly in Helvetica can overrun its cell rule on screen by up to ~6 %.
- **Weight 500** (toasts, the 500-weight dropdown items, and every Statement Writer heading to come) now renders Medium.
