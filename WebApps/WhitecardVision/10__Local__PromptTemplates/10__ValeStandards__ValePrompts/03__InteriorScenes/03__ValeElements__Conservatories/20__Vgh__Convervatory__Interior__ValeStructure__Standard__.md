**VALE GARDEN HOUSES CONSERVATORY ELEMENTS**

**Elements Typical On The Conservatory Framework**
- All framework is hardwood and PAINTED Off White, the white should NOT BE Car crash white, instead a soft white easy on the eyes conveying timeless white conservatory paint.
- Sliding Sash Windows separated by vertical inset panel sections.
- Inset Panelled vertical sections between window and door spans, these vertical panel sections have a inset panel with a classic moulding framing the perimeter of the inset panels.
- Ensure the Conservatory Framework joinery, mullions, transoms, doors, cills, mouldings and roof glazing bars are crisp and prominent.

**SPECIFIC FRAMEWORK ELEMENTS**
**Right Side Conservatory Framework**
- Two Sets of double doors with lower section fielded panels with inset panel with classic conservatory moulding framing the perimeter of the the fielded panel.
- The double door handles are a traditional brass finish.
- The door level handles are a Scroll Lever Type handle.
- Sliding Sash windows with inset panel sections between sat atop a dwarf wall.

 **CONSERVATORY ROOF**
- The Roof is a Monopitched Roof & Spans from the right to the left, the conservatory roof is doubled glazed panels set between the structural roof timbers.
- The Roof timbers have a classic moulding to their underside edges.
- On the left side of the roof at the apex where it meets the wall there are Roof Vents, The roof vents are opening based casements that open to ventilate the apex of the roof. These vents are in their closed positions.
- Along the Apex there is a timber wall plate with a moulded bottom edge that the roof timbers are seated against.
- Around the Eaves above the window and door panels there is a decorative traditional crown moulding profile featuring a sequence of stepped curves and square fillets and staff bead.
- The conservatory Roof Features two lighting hooks on a lighting blocks positioned roughly at the mid span of one of the structural roof timbers.
- Note: there are two different kinds of roof timber, shallower in depth are the glaze bars and thicker in depth are the Structural Roof Supports such as the on the lighting block is affixed to.
