The elevation-plane workflow, inspired by ValeVision's drag-and-position plane and camera.

**Belongs here**

- Measured Elevation: projection and centrelines exact, line looseness capped, decorative ink width kept distinct from geometry.
- Concept Elevation: the same geometry and passes, with stronger secondary-line expression allowed.

**Takes** a plane in the scene. **Gives** an orthographic camera and its ceiling.
