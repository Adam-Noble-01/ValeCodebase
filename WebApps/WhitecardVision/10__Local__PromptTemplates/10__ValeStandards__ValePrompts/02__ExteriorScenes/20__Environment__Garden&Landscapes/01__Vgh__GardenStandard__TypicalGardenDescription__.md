**THE GARDEN & SURROUNDING LANDSCAPE**

**Foreground Garden**
- Patio Area around orangery & house 
- A Manicured Lawn
- Small shrubs.
- Potted Plants on Patio Area

**Patio Area & Outdoor Furniture**
- A slabbed Patio Area around the Orangery & Main House paved in York Stone Slabs.
- Outdoor dining set dressed with classical outdoor high-end British food and wine etc and luxury lifestyle props.
- Outdoor lounging area.

**Background Landscape**
- Trees and British Countryside, should not be the focus of the shot, the main focus should be showing off the Orangery.

**GARDEN VIBE**
- Sophisticated and elegant, but not overly formal.
- A relaxed and inviting atmosphere.
- A sense of luxury and British refinement.
- Aim to show the Orangery off to its best advantage, with the garden and landscape providing a backdrop to the Orangery.
- The garden should portray lifestyle and luxury British countryside living to sell the Vale Orangery Design Scheme to the target audience (Vale Garden Houses Clients).
